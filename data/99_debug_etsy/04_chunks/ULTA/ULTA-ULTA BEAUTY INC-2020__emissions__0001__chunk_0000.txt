Energy & emissions
Our retail stores are the focus of our energy
Up to 99% of stores make use of an energy
(EMS), which helps us actively conserve
annual operating costs.
The EMS precisely controls temperature
and ensures minimal lighting is in use
outside of retail hours. The system also
detects malfunctioning equipment before
it impacts the store environment and
wastes energy. An EMS allows Ulta Beauty
stores to participate in automated demand
response programs by shifting or reducing
electricity usage during peak periods,
helping to stabilize the electric grid. We
also utilize peak load management, or
demand-limiting, strategies to reduce
peak demand and save on utility bills.
