How We Drive Impact:
Underpinning our Impact strategy is a set of levers we employ to drive toward our Impact goals while advancing and
complementing our business strategy.
Business Integration
Responsibility and accountability for implementing our
Impact strategy is integrated and owned across our
Company through many cross functional teams. Our
Impact strategy is approved annually by our Executive
Team, and each of our Impact goals has an Executive
sponsor. Our Board of Directors has oversight of
these activities.
We are working to integrate our Impact ambitions into how
our marketplace platforms are designed and operated, and
how our sellers and buyers experience our marketplaces. By
making it easier for sellers and buyers to understand their
personal and collective impact, we aim to influence more
sustainable practices on the seller side while helping buyers
make more informed decisions. We are also conscious
about using marketing data and marketing images
in responsible and ethical ways.
