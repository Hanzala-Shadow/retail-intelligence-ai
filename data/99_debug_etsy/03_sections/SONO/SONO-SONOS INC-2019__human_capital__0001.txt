CASE STUDY
Supporting the future
female tech talent
Women are consistently
underrepresented in our industry. The
annual Grace Hopper Celebration —
the world’s largest gathering of women
in technology — wants to change that.
For the second year running, we
sent employees to Grace Hopper to
accelerate their careers, get inspired
and meet the next generation of talent.
In 2019 we sponsored attendance
for 13 women from different levels,
departments and offices. Our CEO,
Patrick Spence, also attended to
represent Sonos in recruiting female
tech leaders. At the career and
interview booths, 11 additional Sonos
team members connected with nearly
1,170  high-potential candidates.
