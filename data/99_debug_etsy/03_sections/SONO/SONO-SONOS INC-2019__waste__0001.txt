PERFORMANCE
This year we launched our first portable speaker,
Move. This is powered by a rechargeable
battery designed for 900 charge cycles,
equivalent to about three years of use. When
the battery eventually degrades, we designed
Move so the customer can simply replace it at
home. The easy change feature extends the
life of the product, preventing perfectly good
equipment from being disposed of due to the
difficulty of battery replacement.

Standby power
APPROACH
Sonos speakers are ready to play music and
respond to commands almost instantly. To do
this, our products go into standby when they
aren’t being used. The energy consumed in this
mode is called ‘standby power.’
We are pushing the boundaries of product
design to achieve the minimum standby power
level. As we update existing products and
introduce new ones, we replace old components
(like radio and processor platforms) with efficient
electronics. Ever-smarter software powers only
the functions necessary to activate the speaker.
This delivers the immediate response our
customers expect while reducing the standby
power of our product portfolio.
